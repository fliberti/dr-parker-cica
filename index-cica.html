The Forensic Advantage

Why Standard Medical Records Fail You In Settlement Negotiations

Doctors document treatment, not economic loss. By relying solely on medical notes, you are letting the defense dictate the narrative. We bridge the gap between medical reality and legal valuation.

1. Uncover What Medicine Misses

Standard exams leave invisible injuries behind. Our audit uses proprietary trauma inventories to identify missed diagnoses (pain interference, emotional disorders, loss of independence) that hold massive compensable value under the law.

2. Quantify the Economic Loss

We don't rely on subjective pain descriptors. We apply forensic clinical economics—using AMA Guides and U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics data—to translate loss of function and future medical needs into exact, quantifiable monetary terms.

3. Deliver Bulletproof Evidence

Every data point aligns with Federal Rules of Evidence 702 and Daubert/Frye admissibility standards. We provide you with objective, reproducible proof that destroys defense low-ball tactics in mediation and court.
